Our story

Food that feels generous, elegant, and rooted in Lagos.

Pleasant Satisfaction

A restaurant created for the joy of eating well.

Born in Ogudu, Pleasant Satisfaction was shaped by a simple belief: excellent food should feel both refined and familiar. Our kitchen blends Nigerian classics, continental techniques, grills, snacks, desserts, and cocktails into a menu that suits everyday cravings and memorable occasions.

We care about timing, temperature, plating, and the little gestures that make a guest feel welcome. Whether you dine in, reserve for a celebration, or order from home, the experience is designed to feel calm, clear, and complete.

Mission

To serve flavorful meals with premium hospitality, reliable ordering, and a warm Ogudu dining experience.
